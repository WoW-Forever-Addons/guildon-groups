# Changes

## 0.2.1

- **Zone map:** a request for questing in a zone shows the zone's world map on top of its card, and a piece of it in the list.
- **Quest requests:** the list, the toast and the card say whether the quest is in your quest log or you already did it.
- **Toasts for raids:** raids have their own switch, apart from dungeons; every kind of request can be switched off on its own.
- **Card:** the creator's name is no longer cut off; online or offline stands in the line under it.

## 0.2.0

- **Your roles:** set the role of your first and second spec (window and options, per character). "I'm in" offers the roles that still have a place.
- **Toasts only with a place for you:** a dungeon group has one tank, one healer and three damage dealers; without a free place for one of your roles there is no toast (switch in the options). Raids and all other requests always count.
- **Pictures:** the race picture and class symbol next to every name in the group, the toast and the list; the game's dungeon pictures in the dungeon choice, the list and the card; role symbols.
- **Light on data:** race and sex travel as two or three characters inside the data that is sent anyway, no extra messages.

## 0.1.0

- **First version:** a group finder inside the guild. Post a request for a dungeon or raid, questing in a zone, a quest from your log, an item or anything else, for now or for a day and time.
- **Toasts:** members whose level suits get a small card with "I'm in". Dungeons count from 3 levels under to 12 levels over the last boss. One switch per category, a level rule, sound, chat line, and "Do not disturb" (/gng dnd).
- **Joining:** "I'm in" with your role; the creator sees who is in and invites everyone with one click.
- **The group at a glance:** tank on top, healer second, damage dealers below, names in class colours; a dungeon shows its free places.
- **No stale requests:** "now" runs out after 45 minutes, planned requests 2 hours after their time, and your dungeon request closes when you enter an instance. Planned requests remind you 15 minutes before.
- **Guild chat:** one line for members without the addon, if you want it.
- English and German.
